
# My favorite series/movies
| Title | Genre | Description |
| ----------- | ----------- | ----------- |
| 🧟 World War Z | Action, Sci-Fi | A movie about the spread of a disease that turns people into fast and aggressive zombies. |
| 👑 Game of Thrones | Action, Fantasy | A fantasy movie that is derived from the novel series A Song of Fire and Ice. It contains many fictional elements such as magic and dragons and shows conflicts over a throne. |
| 🌌 Guardians of the Galaxy | Action, Sci-Fi | A superhero franchise from Marvels. It is a series that focuses on a group of outcasts, misfits, and mercenaries who band together to protect their universe from various cosmic threats. |
| 🐼 Kung Fu Panda | Action, Adventure, Comedy | A fictional movie set in a world where animals move like humans. It focuses on a panda named Po who wished to become a kung fu master. Together with his master and friends, they fight whoever threatens the safety of their people. |
| 👊 One Punch Man | Action, Parody, Comedy | An anime where the main character becomes bored due to being the strongest. He wishes to one day meet an opponent that would give him the adrenaline of fighting. |
| 🏴‍☠️ One Piece | Action, Adventure | An anime set in a world of mixed of magic and pirates that follows a young man named Monkey D. Luffy who gained the power of rubber. His ultimate goal is to become the Pirate King and find the treasure called the One Piece. |
| ⚔️ Attack on Titan | Action, Dark Fantasy | A dark fantasy anime where humanity lives within giant walls to protect themselves from mindless titans, mindless man-eating giant humanoid creatures. As the story progresses, the main characters slowly discovery the truths of their world. |
